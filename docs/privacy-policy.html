Tile Merge Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

This policy explains how the Tile Merge app ("the app") handles information. The app is a single-player number puzzle game.

Information the app stores on your device

Your current game, best scores, remaining undos, and whether you bought Premium are saved only on your device. We do not collect, receive, or store this information on any server.

Advertising

The free version of the app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system, and how you interact with ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK, and some US states), the app asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice at any time under Settings → Ad privacy choices.

Learn how Google uses this information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android device settings.

Purchases

The optional Premium purchase is processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details. Google Play tells the app only whether you own Premium.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
